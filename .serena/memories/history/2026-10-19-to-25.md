| 2026-10-21 | `fix/safari-idb-stranded` | **Safari no longer keeps the IndexedDB database of a worker the library terminates** (WebKit 324094, still in Safari 27.0.1; diagnosis and every number in `mem:follow-ups/safari-idb-stranded`, SAFARI-IDB-STRAND and SAFARI-IDB-LIB in `mem:measurements/test-browsers`).
