# Follow-up — three browser tests that no mutation of their subject turns red

**Status: OPEN** — found 2026-10-29 by the falsification campaign of `test/next-task-yields`; the user wants them dealt with (to do: 2, then 1, then 3).

Part of `mem:follow-ups`.

The campaign mutated `src/` once per test touched by the `nextTask()` change and ran the test's file on `chrome · OPFSWriteAheadVFS/sync` (and Firefox where tagged). Seven mutations of ten turned their tests red. Three did not, **and they stay green the same way with `main`'s versions of the files (the `setTimeout` ones)**, so the gap predates `nextTask()`:

1. `abandon.test.ts`, "does not truncate the query the worker has moved on to" — stays green with `if (on !== servingQuery) return;` removed from `interrupt()` in `src/pool.ts` (only "does not stop the live query when named the stale one", which calls `interrupt()` directly, goes red). **Hypothesis, unverified:** the test's premise — a transaction ends leaving a stale transport whose abort listener is still armed — no longer occurs, since `closeOpenStatements()` returns the abandoned generator and `drain`'s finally detaches the listener. Check with a double mutation (the guard plus `closeOpenStatements()`); if confirmed, find a path that still reaches the state, or retire the test as covered by the direct one.
2. `abandon-transaction.test.ts`, "rejects when the callback leaves a next() in flight" — **its documented falsifier is refuted**: deleting `worker.interrupt(transport)` from `closeOpenStatements()` in `src/transaction.ts` leaves it green. **Hypothesis, unverified:** a second path stops the in-flight `next()` — the transaction's merged signal aborts at its death, `tx.chunk` carries it, and the D7 reclaim listener of `chunk()` (`src/queries.ts`) interrupts. Check with a double mutation; then correct the comment and decide whether each path needs a test of its own.
3. `chunk-delivery.test.ts`, "delivers every row to a stream consumer that awaits per row" — has no documented falsifier; a one-slot inbox (`inbox = [data.data]` instead of `inbox.push(data.data)` in `src/pool.ts`) leaves it green on Chrome and Firefox. The mutation may not be the original defect (a lost wake-up while the pool generator sat at its `yield`, 501 of 1001 rows): find the commit that fixed it and replay its inverse. Red: write it as the falsifier. Green: the test no longer reaches its precondition — more rows, smaller chunks, and check that several chunks are in flight.

The campaign's ten mutations, for reuse: `registry.watch` in `chunk()` (abandon-gc ×4, red), `chunk()`'s abort listener (abandon "at the deadline"/"at once", red), the `interrupt()` guard (1 above), both `closeOpenStatements()` call sites (abandon-transaction "commits", red), its `interrupt` (2 above), the one-slot inbox (3 above), `scheduler.acquire('verdict', undefined, …)` and the verdict waiters left out of `shutdown()` (the two `run.test.ts` tests, red), a query-long `Promise.race` in `drain` (four `stream-retention` tests on Firefox, red). Making such a campaign a versioned tool was suggested, not decided.
